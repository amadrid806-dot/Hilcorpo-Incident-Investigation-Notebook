# Design System

## Palette
- Hilcorp Green: `#007A53`
- Charcoal: `#172124`
- Mid Gray: `#697579`
- Light Gray: `#E6EAEB`
- Paper Gray: `#F3F5F5`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography
Uses built-in Helvetica / Helvetica Bold so the PDFs are portable and do not depend on external font files.

## Page conventions
- Portrait orientation
- Header with small Hilcorp logo and section title
- Green divider line
- Clickable top navigation ribbon
- Footer with title, version, and page number
- 5 mm dot grids for freehand notes
- Thin gray rules and low-ink forms for e-ink and printing
